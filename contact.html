Privacy Notice

Waitlist & research data: We collect only the email, scenario, optional name/team/Mac info, rich-text notes, and optional feedback you submit, plus referral/UTM parameters if present. We use it strictly for R1 validation updates and optional 15-minute research conversations—never sold or rented.

R1 product privacy design intentions (concept): Participants review and confirm notes before sharing; voiceprint gating is designed to keep only enrolled teammates’ speech; managers cannot access raw audio; physical mute switch and top status light; local audio processing on Mac with delete control; team content is never used to train models.

Unsubscribe & deletion: Every email includes one-click unsubscribe, and you may request deletion of your waitlist record at any time.
