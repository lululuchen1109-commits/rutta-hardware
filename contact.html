Privacy Notice

What we collect: Only the details you submit through our waitlist or contact form (email address, optional name/organization, Mac model, use case, inquiry message, and optional survey feedback), plus URL campaign attribution (UTM/ref) if present.

How we use it: Strictly to reply to your inquiry or share Rutta Bar milestones, prototype testing invitations, and launch pricing. We never sell or rent your email address.

Hardware privacy: Rutta Bar itself is a USB-C microphone with no internal storage, no account, and no cloud connection.

Unsubscribe & deletion: Every waitlist email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can request deletion of your record at any time.
