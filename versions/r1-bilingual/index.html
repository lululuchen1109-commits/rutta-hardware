Why R1 exists · For 3–15 person teams

The moments that move a team forward happen between meetings.

R1 is designed for the moments teams usually lose: a desk-side decision, a handoff, a quick customer debrief, or the context an AI Agent needs to act correctly.

01 · Desk-side timeline shift
“Let’s push launch from Dec 3 to Dec 10.”

Spoken decisions stop slipping away

Two minutes after a desk chat, both teammates see a drafted change. One click confirms it—updating the plan, shifting dependent tasks, and notifying everyone affected without anyone writing a doc.

02 · Standup promises & handoffs
“I’ll send the revised quote on Monday.”

Commitments turn into owned tasks

Voiceprint attribution knows who volunteered. R1 drafts an action item with the right owner and due date—and closes the loop when someone says it’s sent.

03 · Before & after the meeting
“We picked option B because the client needs SSO first.”

The “why” stays attached to the decision

The real reasoning often happens right after the call ends or walking back from the room. Absent teammates and new hires can look up how and why a choice was made.

04 · Keeping AI Agents in sync
“What’s our current launch date and what’s blocking it?”

Stop retyping verbal updates into prompts

Your Agents can write docs and open tickets, but they don’t know what you just decided out loud. Confirmed decisions flow straight to your docs, trackers, and Agents via MCP, API, and CLI.

How R1 is designed to work

From desk talk to team memory—with zero manual note-taking.

  1. 01Plug in

    Slides into your Mac’s USB-C port. Powered by your laptop—online whenever your Mac is open.

  2. 02Enroll once

    Each consenting teammate records one short voice sample. Leave anytime.

  3. 03Talk normally

    Desk chats, standups, meeting rooms—nothing to start, stop, or charge.

  4. 04Confirm first

    Only conversation participants see the drafted note first. One click shares it.

  5. 05Syncs to your stack

    Confirmed decisions, tasks, and context flow into your tools and AI Agents.

Privacy by design · Six commitments

A work microphone with a clear boundary.

R1 lives on your Mac strictly for work settings—desk and meeting room, never hallways or private time. Below are six privacy commitments that shape the R1 experience:

Light off · Orange mechanical marker visible in the switch track.

  1. 01

    Participants see it first

    Drafted notes are visible only to the people who spoke in that conversation. Nothing enters team knowledge until someone confirms and shares it.

  2. 02

    Designed for enrolled voices only

    Voiceprint gating is designed to keep only enrolled teammates’ speech and discard visitors or non-enrolled colleagues without transcribing or saving.

  3. 03

    Managers never see raw audio

    Admins and managers only ever access confirmed team decisions and tasks—never raw audio recordings.

  4. 04

    Physical mute switch & visible light

    A mechanical aluminum switch cuts input on hardware, and a top status light lets everyone at the table see live status at a glance.

  5. 05

    Local audio processing & delete control

    Audio is intended to be processed locally on your Mac—only text from enrolled teammates syncs, and anyone can delete records they were part of.

  6. 06

    Never used to train AI models

    Your team’s conversations, decisions, and knowledge will never be used to train foundation models.

Questions

Is R1 built or taking pre-orders today?

No. R1 is in early concept validation—the hardware shown here is an industrial design concept and the workflow illustrates how R1 is intended to work. We aren’t collecting payments or pre-orders yet; we’re speaking with co-located teams first to decide whether to build it.

How does voiceprint gating handle visitors or colleagues who haven’t opted in?

Each consenting teammate records one short voice sample once. Our design goal is to process only enrolled teammates’ speech and discard unrecognized voices without transcribing or saving them. Accuracy on very short phrases and larger teams is under active engineering evaluation.

Why a physical Mac accessory instead of just software?

Battery-powered wearables struggle to stay reliably online all day without charging, and running a Mac recording app constantly competes with system mic indicators, sleep states, and video calls. Plugging R1 into USB-C keeps it powered strictly while you’re working at your Mac.

Will our team’s knowledge be locked inside Rutta?

No. Confirmed decisions, action items, and key facts are designed to distribute cleanly via MCP, API, and CLI into the docs, task trackers, chat tools, and AI Agents your team already uses.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

We’ll only reach out with genuine validation milestones—and if you’re on a 3–15 person co-located team, we’d love to invite you to a 15-minute chat (call, video, or email) about the last verbal decision your team had to chase down.
